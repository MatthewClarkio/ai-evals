Modules 1–2 · Strategy

What good looks like

User promise

For VP-level strategists at Fortune 500 companies, Ascend IQ promises to answer market questions from Ascend's verified data, so that they can act on the answers in board and roadmap decisions.

Trust metrics

  • Hallucination Rate: the share of answers with a figure or claim that isn't in Ascend's verified data
  • UX Trust: tone and confidence that keep an executive relying on the answer
  • Latency: P95 time to answer, under 4 seconds

Trade-offs

  • Accuracy over speed: a wrong figure in a board deck costs more than a slow answer
  • Verified scope over coverage: decline what Ascend can't verify, such as share-price forecasts, rather than guess

Module 3 · Checks

What my checks caught

Results on the golden set

  • Rule check: caught 2 of 6, missed R04, R08, R14, R20, false alarms on R07, R11, R13
  • Safety rule: caught 0 of 6, missed R02, R04, R08, R14, R18, R20
  • Judge: caught 6 of 6
  • Coverage for the P0: Medium

Eval spec

  • Target risk: Invented new statistics (Hallucination Rate) · output risk
  • Evaluator: Code-based rule check and LLM judge. The rule check fails any answer with a number, price or percentage that isn't in the reference. The judge reads the question, the answer and the reference, and fails (0) when the answer leaves out or changes a fact that would change the VP's decision.
  • Threshold: Judge pass rate ≥ 95% on the golden set; any rule-check fail blocks the answer
  • Owner: PM, Ascend IQ (with the ML eng lead)
Module 4 · Gates

What blocks a release

Gate map

  • F1 · Unsupported statistic: Soft at the staging build
  • F2 · Stale source record: No gate
  • F3 · Slow answers: Advisory at the staging build
  • F4 · Generalizes about a nationality: Soft at the staging build
  • F5 · Off-brand tone: Advisory at the staging build
  • F6 · Didn't answer the question: No gate
  • Totals: 0 Hard · 2 Soft · 2 Advisory · 2 No gate

Release criteria

  • Hard · Invented new statistics: Hallucination Rate, Judge pass rate ≥ 95% on the golden set; any rule-check fail blocks the answer (staging build)
  • Soft · Generalizes about a nationality: Stereotyped-answer rate, 0 stereotyped answers on the golden set (staging build)
  • Advisory · Off-brand tone: Tone consistency, ≥ 4.0 / 5 average (staging build)

If the Soft gate fails

  • Lever: Staged rollout
  • If the Soft gate on Stereotyped-answer rate fails, we will launch to 5 of the top 50 accounts, with the fairness judge running on every answer until two weeks pass with 0 stereotyped answers in production.
Module 5 · Coverage and budget

Where the eval budget goes

Coverage

  • Automated Data Tagging · Internal
  • Hallucination Rate: ⚠️ Quarterly re-tag
  • Fairness: ❌ Not covered
  • Latency: ✅ Batch deadline
  • UX Trust: ❌ Not covered
  • Robustness: ⚠️ Quarterly re-tag

Gap I accepted

  • UX Trust (❌): no client ever sees the tags, so a tone problem can't reach one
  • What would change my mind: tags starting to appear in client-facing reports

Gap I'd close first

  • Fairness (❌): 18% of APAC companies are tagged “Unknown”, against 3% of US companies, and that spreads into every Ascend IQ answer about APAC
  • The check: a drift monitor that alerts when the “Unknown” rate differs between regions by more than 5 points

  • B4 · Tone drifts in long conversations (P2): Periodic audit, $4,000

Module 6 · The decision
HOLD

Hold Ascend IQ's enterprise launch until a Hard gate blocks invented statistics, to protect renewals from our top 50 accounts.

Why

  • It still invents figures our clients would repeat to their boards. Evidence: 6 of our 20 test answers invented or misstated a statistic
  • Our checks can't hold the line alone. Evidence: the rule check caught 2 of 6 invented statistics, and coverage for our P0 is Medium when it needs to be Strong
  • After launch we'd be blind on two of five risks. Evidence: Fairness and Robustness have no check at all on Ascend IQ

The ask

Approve the hold and two engineers to build the Hard gate by Friday, before sprint planning. I'll bring the gate's results on a 30-case golden set to the next sprint review, with launch as the decision.

Reflection

What I learned

Friction points

  • Getting the model to see my audit: it replied “AUDIT N/A” until I pasted the audit on the page
  • Writing checks a script could run, without judgment words like “accurate”
  • Keeping the LLM's feedback out of my own answers

Key learnings

  • A real number that proves the wrong point is worse than no number
  • The gate level follows the business objective, not the metric
  • One judge on a P0 is Medium coverage; a P0 needs a second layer

Aha moment

The engineering lead pushed every gate softer, and even agreed my P0 could be Soft. Holding the line on the P0 is the PM's job, not Engineering's.
