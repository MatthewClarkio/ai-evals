Lab 1 · 35 minGraded · eval-suite.md

Build Your Output Suite

The 20 answers you test against, each with a verified correct answer, are a golden set: a fixed set of cases you check every change against. Your Module 2 audit is the answer key. A check is good if it fails the rows you failed and passes the rows you passed.

1 · What does it look at?The parts of the row it reads: the question, the answer, the reference.
2 · What counts as a pass?One specific condition you can see in the answer.
3 · What counts as a fail?The condition that breaks it, written so two people would make the same call.
4 · What does it return?1 for pass or 0 for fail. A judge also returns one line of reasoning.
  1. Carry over your P0. Copy your #1 failure from your Module 2 failure taxonomy, and pick the row that shows it.
  2. Write three checks. A rule check, a safety rule and a judge, each answering the four questions.
  3. Run them. Copy the prompt into any LLM, paste your audit-log.md at the end, and send. Paste the reply here to see what each check caught and missed.
  4. Decide and save. Say where your failure was caught and what you'd ship next. Copy the markdown over 03-eval-suites/eval-suite.md, and commit.

Lab 2 · 25 minGraded · eval-spec.md

        
Write Your Eval Spec

        
Turn your top failure into a spec engineering can build against, then explain it to the three teams who need to act on it. If you leave a part out, engineering, design and leadership can each read the spec differently.

      


      
    
        
  1. Fill in the five parts. Your failure, the check that caught it and your Lab 1 counts carry over. Edit them into a spec.

            Step 1 · The five parts

            
1

              
Target risk
What are you testing? The failure from your taxonomy and its trust metric. Say whether it's an output or a trajectory risk.

              
              

              

            


            
2

              
Evaluator
How will you detect it? A code-based check, an LLM judge or human review. For a trajectory risk, name the dimensions you score.

              

              

              
            


            
3

              
Threshold
What is the pass/fail line? The exact number required to ship.

              
              
Your Lab 1 counts appear here.

              

            


            
4

              
Business stakes
Why does it matter? The cost of failure, such as churn, a lawsuit or damage to the brand.

              
            


            
5

              
Owner
Who monitors and enforces it? The person accountable for the decision and sign-off.
