Lab 2 · 20 minGraded · final project deliverable 1

        
Build Your Evaluation Strategy

        
Define what “good” means for Ascend IQ: the promise it makes to users, the three trust metrics that protect it, and the trade-offs you'll accept.

      


      

        


          

            
Step 1 · Section 1 of the canvas
Product Strategy, The Context

            
Read who Ascend IQ is for, what they use it for, and what's at stake. The three fields are filled in from the Final Project Brief. Edit them if you want to sharpen them.

            

              
Scenario · B2B · Ascend Analytics

              
You are the Group Product Manager at Ascend Analytics, a premium B2B market-intelligence platform. The platform is powerful but dense, and engagement is dropping because users struggle to find specific insights quickly. Your team built Ascend IQ, a customer-facing AI agent that answers plain-language questions like “Compare the pricing models of Stripe and Adyen” or “Summarize the negative G2 reviews for Salesforce.”

              

                
$50,000+a year each client pays for verified data

                
Top 50enterprise accounts the CPO wants to unlock next month

                
Q4retention targets at risk; Sales is already promoting the feature

              

            

            
            
            
          


          

            
Step 2 · Section 2 of the canvas
Write your user promise

            
First decide the outcome Ascend IQ has to deliver: how fast, how accurate, how verifiable. Then complete the sentence.

            

              

              

                
                
                
              

              

                A strong [outcome] answers all three:
                Fast · how quickly?
                Accurate · how correct?
                Verifiable · can the VP check the source?
              

            

          


          

            
Step 3 · Section 2 of the canvas
Pick three trust metrics

            
Click three of the five trust metrics, and write one line on why each one protects your promise. 

            

            
          


          

            
Step 4 · Section 3 of the canvas
Commit to two trade-offs

            
Click the two trade-offs you're most likely to face with Ascend IQ's clients, and finish “We prioritize [X] over [Y] because…” for each. Tags show which of your three metrics each one touches.
