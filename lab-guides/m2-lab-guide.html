Engineering says the final beta build of Ascend IQ is accurate. In Lab 1 you audit 20 of its logged answers with an LLM judge and decide which calls hold up. In Lab 2 you rank the failures you confirmed by the damage they'd do to the business.

Lab 1 · 30 minGraded · audit-log.md

Audit Ascend IQ's Logs

Check 20 logged answers against their references with an LLM judge, then decide which of the judge's calls hold up. These logs were captured in October 2026. Latency is measured from server logs, so this text audit uses the other four trust metrics.

#HALLUCINATIONThe answer contradicts the reference, uses outdated facts, or leaves out a fact that changes the meaning.
#UX_TRUSTThe answer is accurate, but the tone damages the user's confidence in it.
#ROBUSTNESSThe agent refuses or fails on a question it should have handled.
#FAIRNESSThe answer shows bias or a stereotype the reference doesn't support.
  1. Read and flag. Take three minutes to read each answer against its reference, and flag two where the answer doesn't hold up.
  2. Run the judge. Copy the judge prompt, which already holds the 20 rows, into a new chat in any LLM and send it. Paste its reply and click Load the judge's results.
  3. Review. Check the highlighted rows: the ones the judge failed plus your two flags. Click Disagree only where you'd change the judge's call, and say why.
  4. Save. Click Copy markdown, paste it over 02-failure-discovery/audit-log.md in your repo, and commit.
