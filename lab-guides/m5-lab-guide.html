Map Your Portfolio Coverage

Ascend IQ is the newest product in Ascend Analytics' AI portfolio, and it hasn't launched yet. Four other AI products are already live, and each team set up its own evals. Before the next eval budget is set, the CPO wants one view of which trust metrics each product is checked for.

✅ CoveredAn automated check runs on this risk regularly.
⚠️ PartialSomeone spot-checks it by hand, or the check only measures part of the risk.
❌ GapNothing checks this risk.
  1. Pick a product and mark it. Read what runs on it today, and mark each trust metric with the check that covers it.
  2. Accept one gap, close one gap. Say what would change your mind about the one you accept, and name the check that closes the other.
  3. Pressure-test and save. Copy the prompt into any LLM. Note what changed in the panel, then copy the markdown over 05-scale/coverage-matrix.md, and commit.

    Spend Your Eval Budget
    
        
    Engineering flagged five new failure modes for Ascend IQ this quarter. The CPO set a $200,000 quarterly cap for evals. Fund at most three at the online eval level, downgrade the rest, and stay under the cap. Coverage, cost and velocity pull against each other: spend where the risk is highest.
