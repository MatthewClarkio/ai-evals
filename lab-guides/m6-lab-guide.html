Write Your Ship-or-Hold Memo

Executives read the first line and decide whether to keep going. The Pyramid Principle puts the recommendation first, so the call is clear even if they read nothing else. There's no right answer to ship or hold. What matters is that clear evidence backs up your call.

The AnswerYour call and the reason, in one sentence.
The ArgumentsTwo or three main points that don't overlap.
The EvidenceThe number from your repo that proves each point.
The AskThe decision you need, and by when.
  1. Gather your numbers. Paste your files from Modules 2 to 5. The page pulls out the numbers you can cite.
  2. Make the call. Pick Ship or Hold, and write one sentence with your call and the reason.
  3. Back it up. Two or three main points, each with the number that proves it. Then the ask: what the CPO approves, and by when.
  4. Pressure-test and save. Copy the prompt into any LLM. Note what changed, then copy the markdown over 06-culture/ship-hold-memo.md, and commit.
